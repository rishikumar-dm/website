THE SERVICE, EXPLAINED

A facial that answers a clear skin-care need

A facial is time set aside for a complete skin-care sequence. Cleansing removes surface impurities, exfoliation refines the feel of the skin where suitable, and treatment products add the selected care step. A finishing mask or moisturiser brings the appointment together. The aim is a refreshed appearance and a comfortable, cared-for feel.

NeoFair’s skin-care range includes Look Pure Cleanup and named facial options such as Clear Balance, Aquatherm, Power C+, Power Oxygen and Power Hyaluronic. Masks, de-tan services and men’s skin care extend the range. Different options place emphasis on hydration, surface shine, nourishment or a fresher-looking finish rather than offering one facial for every skin type.

01 / EXPLORE THE SERVICES

A clearer guide to facial care.

A cleanup provides focused surface care. A full facial adds a more complete treatment sequence, while masks and de-tan care serve specific finishing purposes. The choice depends on the skin’s current condition and the type of care required.

01

Cleanups & cleansing

A cleanup is a focused appointment for cleansing and refreshing the skin. It removes surface oil, residue and impurities, with suitable exfoliation helping improve the feel of the surface. NeoFair’s Look Pure Cleanup sits within this category. A cleanup is useful when the main need is a straightforward refresh rather than a longer facial sequence. It can support a regular skin-care routine without being presented as treatment for a medical skin condition.

02

Facial care options

A facial combines several skin-care steps in one appointment. Cleansing prepares the surface, selected products provide the main care phase, and a mask or finishing moisturiser completes the service. NeoFair’s named options include Clear Balance, Aquatherm, Power C+, Power Oxygen and Power Hyaluronic. The choice of products and intensity varies across the range, with the overall goal of improving the skin’s feel and refreshed appearance.

03

Masks & add-ons

A facial mask stays on the skin for a defined part of the appointment and provides a focused care step. A nourishing mask centres on softness and comfort; a shine-control option is aimed at surface oiliness; a brightening option works towards a fresher-looking finish. NeoFair’s range includes Rich Nourishing, Pure Shine Control and Brightening Glow. These are additional care choices that complement the main service rather than replace cleansing or everyday skin care.

04

De-tan & preparation

De-tan care focuses on the appearance of surface dullness and uneven-looking tan through cleansing and exfoliating products. The service may cover the face or a selected body area, depending on the appointment. Its purpose is a refreshed appearance and more even-looking surface tone. It does not permanently change natural skin colour or replace sun protection. Skin that is irritated or recently sunburnt needs time to recover before exfoliating care.

05

Skin care for men

Men’s cleanups and facials provide dedicated care for surface oil, dryness and dullness. Cleansing around the beard and attention to recently shaved areas are part of choosing an appropriate approach. The service can sit alongside a haircut and grooming routine, creating a more complete appointment. The underlying purpose remains the same: a thoroughly cleansed surface, suitable care products and a refreshed finish.

IN DETAIL

Understand the service before you choose.

How a facial appointment works

A facial generally starts with cleansing to remove surface oil, sunscreen and residue. Suitable exfoliation can then lift built-up surface cells, followed by treatment products and a finishing mask or moisturiser. The products and length of the sequence depend on the named service and how the skin feels on the day. NeoFair’s range includes Look Pure Cleanup, Clear Balance, Aquatherm, Power C+, Power Oxygen and Power Hyaluronic. Those names identify menu choices, but a name by itself does not prove a particular active ingredient or clinical effect. A cleanup has a shorter, more focused purpose than a complete facial ritual.

Matching the care to skin feel and event timing

Skin that looks shiny can still feel tight, so surface oil and hydration are considered separately. A gentle care approach may suit skin that feels reactive, while a more thorough cleanse may appeal when residue and oil are the main issue. Masks such as NeoFair’s Pure Shine Control and Rich Nourishing bring different finishing textures; Brightening Glow aims at a fresher-looking appearance. Before an important function, familiar care usually makes scheduling simpler than trying a stronger treatment immediately before makeup. Irritated or sunburnt skin needs recovery rather than extra exfoliation.

What facials can and cannot do

A salon facial can improve the feel and appearance of the skin for a period, especially through cleansing and moisturising. It cannot permanently change natural complexion or diagnose the cause of acne, scarring or pigmentation. De-tan services address the look of surface dullness and uneven tanning; daily sun protection matters to longer-term skin appearance. A standard facial is also separate from advanced peels, microneedling or device-assisted procedures, which have different suitability and aftercare requirements. Men’s skin care follows the same general principles while accounting for beard and shaving areas.

FIND YOUR FIT

Two ways to approach your visit.

For regular skin care

A cleanup or gentle facial offers cleansing and a refreshed finish. The choice turns on how much time and how many care steps you want in the appointment.

For a wedding or event

Consider the date, any sensitivity and what makeup will follow. A hydrating or nourishing facial and a de-tan service are separate choices rather than an automatic package.

What shapes the quote

A cleanup, named facial, mask add-on and de-tan service have different product steps and appointment scopes. The current price depends on the selected service and any combination with grooming or occasion preparation.

05 / BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Useful answers.

Clear explanations of the differences between services, finishes and care.

Is a facial the same as a cleanup?+

A cleanup concentrates on cleansing and surface refreshment. A facial usually includes a longer sequence with additional treatment products, a mask or other finishing care.

Does oily skin still need hydration?+

Yes. Surface oil and hydration are different characteristics. Skin can appear oily while feeling dehydrated, so a lighter care approach may still include moisturising steps.

Will de-tan care permanently change my skin colour?+

No. De-tan services aim to improve the appearance of dullness and uneven-looking tan. They do not permanently change the skin’s natural colour.

Can a facial remove acne scars?+

A standard salon facial is for cleansing and cosmetic care. Acne scars require a separate assessment and may need clinical treatment; they are not removed by an ordinary facial.

Will one facial remove pigmentation or acne scars?+

No. A salon facial provides cosmetic cleansing and care. Pigmentation and scars have different causes and may require assessment and procedures outside a routine facial.

Should I book a facial on the wedding day?+

That depends on the exact service and your skin. A familiar, gentle routine is easier to plan around makeup than trying a new exfoliating treatment on the day.

Does de-tan treatment whiten natural skin?+

No. It aims to refresh the appearance of tanning and dullness at the surface. Natural skin colour is not permanently changed.
