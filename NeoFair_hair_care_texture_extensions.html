THE SERVICE, EXPLAINED

Choose care, texture or added length for the right reason

Dryness, frizz, lack of softness and a wish for extra length are different reasons to visit a salon. Hair conditioning improves the feel and manageability of the lengths. A texture service changes the way the hair sits, while extensions add hair to create length or fullness. Understanding these differences makes the service range easier to navigate.

NeoFair’s hair-care menu includes wash-and-condition services, deep nourishment, hair rituals, scalp care, bond strengthening, keratin and chemical texture options. Hair extensions form a separate part of the range. Each service has a different process and maintenance pattern, with the choice shaped by the hair’s current condition and previous processing.

01 / EXPLORE THE SERVICES

Care, smoother texture or added length.

A conditioning ritual works on the feel of the hair. Keratin and other texture services create a more lasting change in its finish. Extensions create visible length or density. These options can support different goals within a complete hair-care routine.

01

Wash, conditioning & hair spa

A salon hair wash cleanses the scalp and removes oil and styling residue. Conditioner improves slip through the lengths, making detangling easier and the hair feel smoother. Deep conditioning and hair-spa rituals provide a more intensive treatment step for dry or rough-feeling hair. NeoFair’s range includes curl nourishment and deep-conditioning options, supporting softness and manageability without changing the haircut or permanently straightening the natural texture.

02

Scalp-focused care

Scalp care concentrates on the skin beneath the hair and the freshness of the roots. Cleansing and selected care products form the basis of the service. NeoFair includes anti-dandruff and anti-hairfall-labelled options within its scalp range. A salon routine can support grooming and scalp comfort, but persistent flakes, inflammation or shedding may have causes that require a dermatologist. A scalp-care label alone does not establish a medical hair-regrowth treatment.

03

Bond and nourishment rituals

Nourishment and bond care address different aspects of processed hair. Conditioning rituals improve softness and combability; bond-strengthening systems are designed to support hair affected by chemical processing. Colour-lock care is tailored to the routine of coloured hair. NeoFair also includes richer hair-and-scalp rituals within its menu. These services can improve the finished feel, although they cannot make heavily processed hair behave exactly like untreated hair.

04

Keratin, smoothening & straightening

Keratin services aim to reduce frizz and make styling more manageable. Smoothening produces a sleeker finish, while straightening and rebonding can create a stronger change in the natural texture. The exact method and result depend on the treatment system. Chemical services require attention to previous bleach, colour and texture treatments because already processed hair may be less suited to further change. New growth retains its natural texture, creating a need for maintenance over time.

05

Extensions & length planning

Hair extensions add extra strands or sections to create length, fullness or both. A convincing result depends on matching the colour and texture, distributing the added hair and blending it into the existing haircut. The attachment method influences how the hair is washed, brushed and styled, as well as how often maintenance is needed. Extensions add visible hair; they do not change the growth rate of the natural hair underneath.

IN DETAIL

Understand the service before you choose.

Conditioning, scalp care and bond care have separate roles

Shampoo cleanses oil and residue from the scalp; conditioner works mainly on the hair lengths to improve slip. A hair spa usually adds a longer conditioning ritual for softness and a smoother feel. Scalp-focused services address cleansing and comfort at the roots, while bond systems are used in relation to chemical processing. NeoFair’s menu includes colour-lock, deep nourishment and bond-strengthening options alongside anti-dandruff and anti-hairfall-labelled services. A salon scalp appointment does not diagnose the cause of persistent shedding or treat a medical scalp disorder. Where symptoms continue, clinical assessment matters more than a menu name.

How smoothing differs from straightening

Keratin and smoothening services are commonly chosen to reduce visible frizz and make a sleeker finish easier to style. Straightening or rebonding aims for a more pronounced change in the hair’s natural pattern. Different formulations, processing methods and heat steps lead to different results and aftercare. Previously lightened or chemically treated hair may not tolerate the same process as unprocessed hair. The treated lengths and new hair growing at the roots will not have an identical texture forever, so maintenance becomes part of the decision. A conditioning ritual alone does not permanently straighten hair.

What makes extensions look convincing

Extensions can add discreet fullness to the sides and ends, or create a more dramatic change in length. The shade must relate to the natural hair and any existing colour; texture and placement matter as much as length. A heavy or poorly distributed addition can be difficult to blend, especially with a blunt short cut. NeoFair lists extension method and price on request, which is appropriate because fitting, maintenance and removal depend on the chosen system. Extensions alter the visible amount of hair. They do not cause the wearer’s own hair to grow faster and should be taken off using the method suited to the attachment.

FIND YOUR FIT

Two ways to approach your visit.

For softer hair now

A wash, conditioning or nourishment ritual fits a brief centred on feel, slip and styling ease. A haircut can remove split ends; conditioning cannot fuse them permanently.

For a lasting visual change

Keratin, smoothening, straightening or extensions need more planning. Hair history and the intended finish affect technique, upkeep and the appointment scope.

What shapes the quote

A conditioning ritual, texture process and extension fitting have different methods and costs. Hair length, previous processing, product choice and extension method or amount affect the quote. Maintenance or removal may be a separate appointment.

03 / SKILL IN THE DETAILS

Technique makes the difference.

Hair-care work begins with recognising what the hair needs and what it can tolerate. Product choice, careful sectioning and controlled processing matter in texture services. With extensions, colour matching and balanced placement are central to a finish that blends into the natural hair.

05 / BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Useful answers.

Clear explanations of the differences between services, finishes and care.

Is a hair spa the same as keratin?+

No. A hair spa focuses on conditioning and the feel of the hair. Keratin is a smoothing service intended to reduce frizz and change the styling finish for a longer period.

Does straightening change the hair that grows later?+

No. The treated lengths retain the effect of the service, while new growth follows the natural hair pattern. This creates a visible difference at the roots over time.

Do extensions always mean very long hair?+

No. Extensions can add fullness at a similar length, extra length, or both. Placement and blending determine the final shape.

Can conditioning permanently repair split ends?+

Conditioning can improve softness and reduce friction, but it does not permanently join split ends. Trimming removes the split portion, while gentle care helps limit further wear.

Will a hair spa stop hair fall?+

A hair spa is a conditioning service. Hair shedding has many possible causes, so persistent or sudden loss should be assessed by an appropriate clinician.

Can I colour and straighten my hair in the same period?+

The order and spacing depend on previous processing, hair condition and the exact products. Combining chemical services without assessing the hair can increase the risk of breakage.

How long do extensions stay in?+

The answer depends on the attachment method, natural hair growth and maintenance schedule. The salon must specify the method before a realistic upkeep plan can be given.
