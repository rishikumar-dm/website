— How This Began

A different kind of practice, on purpose.

IV League was founded in 2024 after years of watching the same pattern inside larger practices — services pushed on volume, plans written in five-minute intake windows, follow-through outsourced or forgotten. The result was work that looked like everyone else's, and clients who never felt actually seen.

We built this studio to work differently. Fewer clients on the schedule, longer appointments on the calendar, and every plan written by the person who actually provides the treatment. It's slower, quieter, and deliberately harder to scale. That's the point.

— How We Practice

Four ideas we refuse to compromise.

Every part of this studio — the room, the schedule, the pricing, the follow-through — was designed around these four. If any of them isn't true on a given day, we get to the bottom of why.

i. Restraint

Small changes, done well.

The most defining aesthetic and wellness work is the work no one can pinpoint. We treat "less" as the harder, more skilled path — and we plan every result around it. If a treatment isn't right for you today, we tell you so.

ii. Evidence

What's supported, not what's trending.

Every protocol we run — from GLP-1 dosing to peptide selection to injectable placement — is grounded in current clinical evidence, not in whatever is selling on social. If it isn't backed, we don't offer it.

iii. Discretion

Your visit is yours.

Private consultation rooms. No shared waiting areas. No unsolicited follow-up. What we discuss doesn't leave the room, and what you decide to do isn't anyone's business but yours.

iv. Time

Longer appointments, always.

We deliberately book fewer clients than we could. Consultations are thirty minutes, treatments are booked with real margin, and follow-up is a call — not a form. The work costs more time. It's worth it.

— The Studio

A room, not a lobby.

Our studio on the West Loop was designed to feel more like a private practice than a med spa. Warm materials, natural light, a single consultation room and a single treatment room — used by one client at a time.

There is no shared waiting area, no reception counter, and no queue. Clients are greeted by name, walked back to the room, and given the appointment they were promised — without a checkout desk between them and their car.

This is deliberate. The physical space is part of the treatment.

— Who We Serve

Women and men, equally at home here.

Our client base is deliberately split — roughly half women, half men, aged 28 to 68. The practice was built to feel as comfortable for men as for women, which is a design choice, not an afterthought.

Common reasons clients come to IV League for the first time:

  • Fatigue and depletion — clients looking for medical-grade IV therapy to recover from illness, travel, hard training, or a demanding season.
  • Weight that hasn't moved — clients considering GLP-1 medication and wanting to run it as a real medical program, not a mail-order pen.
  • Aesthetic maintenance — clients who've had Botox or filler somewhere else and want a more restrained hand for the year ahead.
  • Skin that's been neglected — clients ready to commit to a real sequenced skin plan rather than a one-off facial.
  • A second opinion — clients who've been recommended aggressive treatments elsewhere and want to hear a more conservative view before deciding.

If any of these sound like your situation — or if none of them do but you're curious — the consultation is complimentary. Thirty minutes with the provider, a written plan, no pressure to book anything on the way out.
